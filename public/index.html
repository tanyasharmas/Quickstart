Model Temperature and Sampling

A model predicts one token at a time, and each token is a probability spread over many possible tokens. Temperature and sampling decide how the next token is picked from the spread.

  1. A model gives probability for every possible next token. It does not output one word. For the next tojen it produces ranked spread, for example
cut off 55%
vendor 25%
network 15%
unknown 5%

  1. Sampling picks one token from the spread. The tool rolls a weighted die. A 55% is chosen about 55 times in 100 runs, not everytime

  2. Temperature reshapes the spread before the roll. Low temperature makes the most likely token much more dominant. High temperature flattens the spread so unlikely tokens get picked more often At temperature 0, the tool always takes the top token

What is an embedding?

An embedding is a way of turning text (or images, audio, etc.)into a list of numbers, called a vector, so that things with similar meanings end up with similar numbers.

For example, “I forgot my login credentials” and “How do I reset my password” share almost no words but their embeddings point in nearly the same direction. A keyword search would miss the match, while embedding search finds it.

Language Models

LLM vs SLM

What is Large Language Model?

Large Language Model (LLMs) are statistical models of language. They extract patterns and relationships in massive amounts of text and build mathematical representation of how words relate to each other.

Frontier Models

A Frontier Model is a highly capable, general-purpose AI model at or near the leading edge of AI development. Unlike narrower models designed for specific tasks, frontier models can perform a broad range of complex tasks and generally require substantial data and computing resources to develop. As AI technology advances, what qualifies as a frontier model may shift.
