# LLM vs SLM

# What is Large Language Model?
Large Language Model (LLMs) are statistical models of language. They extract patterns and relationships in massive amounts of text and build mathematical representation of how words relate to each other. 


# Frontier Models
A Frontier Model is a highly capable, general-purpose AI model at or near the leading edge of AI development. Unlike narrower models designed for specific tasks, frontier models can perform a broad range of complex tasks and generally require substantial data and computing resources to develop. As AI technology advances, what qualifies as a frontier model may shift.

# Open Weight Models
An Open-Weight Model is an AI model whose core components are publicly released, allowing anyone to download it. This lets users run the model on their own computers, study how it works, and even modify it for their own specific needs. 


# Open Source Models
An Open-Source Model is an AI model whose weights, source code, training data—or a detailed, auditable account of that data—and development tools are available for others to download, run, inspect, reproduce, modify, and improve. Unlike an open-weight model, it reveals more than the model’s learned parameters, enabling meaningful research, auditing, and accountability.
